The philosophy

A little magic.

Not simply clothes. A collection of alternate worlds, imagined for people who love a little drama.

01 / OUR STORY

We believe in the power of transformation.

Masquerade & Moon is a concept costume atelier inspired by the romance of historical fashion and the thrill of becoming someone else for a night.

From gothic gowns to regal silhouettes, the wardrobe is designed as an invitation: discover a character, dress the part, and make the ordinary feel extraordinary.
