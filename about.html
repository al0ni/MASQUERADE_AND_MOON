OUR PROJECT

We built this site to showcase our skills.

This website was created as our Front-End Assignment #1 project at Astana IT University. Since we are developers and not actual costume shop owners, we designed this fictional studio concept to demonstrate our technical abilities.

Our goal was to practically apply HTML structure, semantic tags, forms, tables, external CSS, typography, and responsive design within a complete, premium-looking website.
