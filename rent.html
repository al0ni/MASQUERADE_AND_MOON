COMMON QUESTIONS

Before the curtain rises.

Can I actually rent a costume here?

Not yet. Masquerade & Moon is a fictional storefront created as an interactive website demo. Rental requests are saved only in your browser.

What does the listed price mean?

Prices are illustrative amounts in Kazakhstani tenge for a three-day costume rental. They are not real offers.

Where are my requests stored?

In your device's localStorage. This is a front-end simulation without a backend, payment processing or real booking service.

Can I save my favorite looks?

Yes. Tap the heart on any costume, then use the saved-only filter or your profile to revisit it.
