RENTAL TERMS

The little details
matter.

Rental period

Standard rental is 3 days. Need it longer? Tell us in advance and we will calculate an extended rate.

Deposit

A refundable deposit is required at pickup. The exact amount depends on the costume and accessories.

Care

Please keep costumes away from food, open flames, pets and permanent makeup products.

Late return

Late returns may affect the next customer's booking, so please contact us if your plans change.

BEFORE PICKUP

Your checklist.

  1. Bring your reservation confirmation.
  2. Check the costume size before leaving.
  3. Try all accessories with the outfit.
  4. Keep the return date somewhere visible.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Need to know more?

Do I need to wash the costume before returning?

No, professional dry cleaning is included in your rental fee. Please do not attempt to wash the costumes yourself to avoid damaging the delicate fabrics.

What happens if I accidentally damage a piece?

Minor wear and tear is expected. However, significant damage (like large tears or permanent stains) will be deducted from your deposit.

Can I try the costume on before renting?

Since this is a fictional student project, unfortunately no. But in a real scenario, we would offer fitting sessions at our Astana studio!
